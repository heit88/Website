Overview

The MOTOVICTUS 7/8" Steering Stem Mount is a precision-machined replacement for your stock steering stem bolt. It threads directly into your triple clamp and provides a standard 7/8" diameter mounting cylinder, the same interface used by a wide range of handlebar accessories, positioned at the front of your bike where it's protected under the bars.

Whether you're mounting a map switch, GPS navigator, phone mount, action camera, intercom unit, auxiliary lighting switch, or a heated grip controller, the steering stem mount gives you a solid, low-profile anchor point that tucks your gear away from brush strikes, tip-overs, and trail debris. If your accessory clamps to a 7/8" bar, it'll work here.

The mount features a 13/16" hex drive for easy installation and removal with a standard wrench. All surfaces have a MIL-spec coating for superior corrosion resistance and long-term durability in harsh offroad conditions.

Customers who also need additional handlebar clearance to fit their accessory under the bars should consider pairing this mount with our 10mm Bar Riser Kit, or purchase both together as the Under-Bar Accessory Mount Kit.
