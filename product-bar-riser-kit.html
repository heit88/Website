Overview

The MOTOVICTUS 10mm Bar Riser Kit raises your handlebars 10mm using a pair of precision-machined aluminum risers that slot in between your triple clamp and bar mounts. The 10mm of additional height creates the clearance needed to mount accessories under the bars, whether you're pairing with our Universal 7/8" Steering Stem Mount or working with your own setup.

The risers are designed to work with your stock triple clamps and retain the vibration damping of your factory rubber mounts, while providing a secure, rattle-free fit with tight tolerances and a hard-wearing MIL-spec coating.
