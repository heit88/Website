About MOTOVICTUS

Engineered by riders, for riders.

Our Story

Founded by brothers John and Casey Heit, MOTOVICTUS combines decades of dirt bike riding with mechanical engineering expertise. We design and craft high-quality off-road parts that make riding easier, more fun, and more reliable. Built with attention to detail and engineered to exceed OEM standards, our parts are made to perform on long rides and tough trails, so you can focus on the ride, not the bike.

Engineering Excellence

Every part is designed with precision and tested rigorously to ensure it exceeds expectations.

Rider-Tested

We ride what we build. Our products are proven on real trails in real conditions.

Customer Focused

We're here to support riders with quality products and responsive customer service.

Based in Salt Lake City, Utah

Surrounded by some of the best off-road terrain in the country, we have endless opportunities to test and refine our products in challenging conditions.
