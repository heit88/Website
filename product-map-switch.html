Overview

The Motovictus Map Switch Relocation Kit moves your OEM map switch from the top of the handlebars to a protected position underneath, reducing the risk of damage. This simple upgrade saves your expensive switch at a fraction of the cost of a new one, while still allowing for easy accessibilty to switch maps.

The switch mount replaces the stock steering stem bolt to provide a mount for the map switch. The mount provides a 7/8" cylinder with a 13/16" hex. Each kit includes a precision-machined switch mount and a pair of 10 mm bar risers that provide the clearance needed for relocation. The risers retain your factory rubber mounting for vibration damping and incorporate clocking features to ensure perfect alignment every time.

All components have MIL-spec coatings for superior corrosion resistance and wear protection.

Designed to fit KTM, Husqvarna, and GasGas models equipped with an OEM handlebar-mounted map switch. Compatible with stock triple clamps and bar mounts when used with the included 10 mm risers.

  • KTM 2024+ 150/250/300/350/450 XC-W/XCF-W Models
  • KTM 2023+ 125/250/300/350/450 XC/SX/XC-F/SX-F Models

Important Note

If purchasing the switch mount by itself, a bar riser that raises the bar mount at up from the triple clamp 10mm must be used. Lower bar raisers will require the bar mount to be modified to achieve sufficient clearance.
