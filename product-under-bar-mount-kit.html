Overview

The MOTOVICTUS Under-Bar Accessory Mount Kit is the complete solution for anyone who wants to relocate a handlebar-mounted accessory to a protected position under the bars. Whether you're protecting an OEM map switch from damage, mounting a GPS navigator, tucking a phone mount out of harm's way, or relocating any other 7/8" handlebar accessory, this kit has everything you need.

The kit includes the Universal 7/8" Steering Stem Mount, a precision-machined replacement for your stock steering stem bolt that provides a standard 7/8" mounting interface, paired with a set of 10mm Bar Risers that raise the handlebars to create the clearance needed to fit accessories underneath. The risers retain your factory rubber mounting inserts for vibration damping and incorporate clocking features to maintain perfect bar alignment.

This kit works for mounting map switches, GPS and navigation computers, phone mounts, auxiliary lighting switches, and more. If your accessory clamps to a 7/8" bar and you'd rather have it protected under the bars instead of exposed on top, this kit is the cleanest way to get it safely mounted.

All components have MIL-spec coatings for superior corrosion resistance and wear protection in the harshest offroad conditions.
